Event Overview

An Evening to Share

For the past few years, many of you have met me through my busking performances across Japan. Some of you stopped to listen on the street. Others found my music through a short video online.

This evening is different.

It’s a chance to slow down, to sit together in one room, and experience the songs that have shaped my life, the stories behind the journey, and the memories we’ve unknowingly built together.

Whether you’ve been here from the very beginning or this is our first time meeting, I hope you leave feeling like you spent the evening with an old friend.
